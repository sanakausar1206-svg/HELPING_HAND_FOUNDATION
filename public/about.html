Who We Are

Helping Hands Foundation is a sample NGO website created for a college project. It aims to help people in need and encourage social work.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a better community where everyone gets equal opportunities and support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support people through education, food distribution, cleanliness drives, and environmental awareness.
